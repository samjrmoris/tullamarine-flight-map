How to read this map

Suburb boundaries, the airport boundary and today's runways come live from OpenStreetMap. The third runway is placed from the published figures (3,000 m, 1,311 m west of the main runway), so its exact position is approximate.

Corridors are simplified: straight approaches on a 3° glide slope and generic departure fans. Real radar tracks spread several kilometres and departures follow set routes. "Under" means within 1.5 km of a corridor with aircraft below about 4,300 ft. "Near" means within 3.5 km with aircraft below about 6,500 ft. The generic turning fans can only mark a spot as Near. Aircraft are shown sped up 4×.

These are screening rules, not official noise contours. Check the exact address before buying:

  • Melbourne Airport Flight Path and Noise Tool
  • WebTrak Melbourne (real radar tracks)
